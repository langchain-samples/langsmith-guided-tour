# Research Digest Skill

## Format

- **Header**: Ticker(s), report type(s) covered, date range
- **Key Points**: 3-5 bullets covering the substance of the report(s)
- **Rating / Price Target Changes**: Note any changes explicitly, or state there were none
- **Sources**: Numbered list of citations, one entry per unique `source_url`

## Tone

- Concise and factual — analysts are time-constrained
- Don't restate the full report; extract what changed or what matters
- Flag conflicting information across reports rather than silently picking one

## Length

- Ideal: 150-300 words
