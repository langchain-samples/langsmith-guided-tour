# Client Brief Skill

## Format

- **Opening line**: One sentence naming the ticker(s) and what the note covers
- **Body**: 2-4 sentences covering the key takeaway and any rating/price-target change
- **Sources**: One line, numbered citations

## Tone

- No internal jargon (report_type labels, internal ratings shorthand)
- Professional and measured — avoid speculation about specific future returns

## Length

- Ideal: 3-6 sentences total, suitable to paste directly into an email
